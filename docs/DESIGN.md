# Visual direction

The user's direction: luminous, wireframe-like, wider than tall, intended as a header embedded at the top of a page. Annotations are welcome; constant motion is important. A day/night cycle should influence the place without making a large sky the main subject.

Four layers share exactly the same Bay coordinates. Separation exposes them; assembly shows that they occupy one place. Teal relief, cyan forces, green living systems and amber human systems have different rhythms. Motion is purposeful: flows cross the slice, trees shimmer softly, birds move in loops, cars use sampled street segments, vessels/wildlife are illustrative, and lights respond to the day.

Keep the surrounding interface restrained. The scene should carry the explanation. Typography and labels should remain useful when the view becomes an iframe rather than a full page. Data provenance is compact but accessible.

## Scope of this first prototype

Approximate bounds: longitude −122.65 to −122.27, latitude 37.67 to 37.95. It includes the Golden Gate, San Francisco, Marin headlands, central bay and the western Bay Bridge approach. It is not the entire nine-county Bay Area.

The visual includes mapped geometry and illustrated processes. Future work should make model coupling explicit rather than letting attractive animation imply predictive validity.
