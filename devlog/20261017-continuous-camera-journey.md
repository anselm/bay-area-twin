# Give the flight a continuous geography

**October 17, 2026**

The close bridge shots worked, but transitions could reverse the viewing angle abruptly. Replaced the disconnected circuit with a coherent journey: observe Treasure Island, cross the Bay Bridge westbound into SF, circle the existing Transamerica model, cruise the waterfront through Golden Gate, climb out and look back at the city, then return over the Bay.

The flight and its observation path are authored separately. Added a damped, rate-limited orientation so interpolated observation targets cannot whip the camera around when they pass close to its position. On resuming after manual exploration, orientation starts from the visitor's actual view. The route begins on its own opening shot instead of drifting from an unrelated overview. Bridge centerlines, close zoom, banking and the speed multiplier remain.
